# Tracelyt (working name) — Harness Reliability Control Plane

Research and strategy repository for a startup thesis: reliability infrastructure for autonomous AI agents and their harnesses.

- `docs/hrcp/` — the three source-of-truth documents (HRCP-00 product constitution, HRCP-01 technical architecture, HRCP-02 build/validation/commercialization plan).
- `docs/research/00-cofounder-assessment.md` — **start here.** Cofounder-level assessment of the thesis against the market as of October 2026, with a 90-day plan and revised kill criteria.
- `docs/research/01` to `04` — evidence reports behind the assessment: incumbent teardown, emerging startups, standards and coding-agent instrumentation feasibility, market and buyer evidence.

Note: the working name collides with Evidently AI's `tracely` OpenTelemetry library and other products; see the assessment, section 3.8.
