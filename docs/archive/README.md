# Archive — provenance, not source of truth

These documents are retained as the record of how the plan was derived. They are read-only. The source of truth for building the company is `docs/plan/`.

- `hrcp/` — HRCP-00, 01, 02: the original product constitution, architecture and build plan (30 September 2026). Superseded by PLAN-11 (principles and decisions), PLAN-12 (architecture baseline) and PLAN-04 (roadmap). Section citations of the form "HRCP-00 §14" in the plan refer to these files.
- `research/` — the October 2026 cofounder assessment and the four evidence reports (incumbents, emerging startups, standards and instrumentation, market and buyer evidence). Every factual claim in the plan traces to these files. Citations of the form "research track 3" refer to `research/03-...`.

Do not edit these. If a fact changes, update the plan and record the change in PLAN-11 §6.
